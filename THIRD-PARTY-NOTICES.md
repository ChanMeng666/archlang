## OpenPlan3D

`src/import/openplan3d-model.ts`, `src/import/openplan3d-rooms.ts` and
`src/import/openplan3d-catalog.ts` (built into `dist/`) adapt the file validators, the RoomPlan
interchange reader, the room detection and the furniture catalogue sizes of OpenPlan3D,
<https://github.com/laanlabs/openPlan3D>, commit `98f9cb467e4045c6abc5d9880ada1f03dff50c68`, for
`arch import --from openplan3d`.
