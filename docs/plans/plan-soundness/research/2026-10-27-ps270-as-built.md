# PS2.70 as built: `W_GARAGE_TOO_NARROW` judges depth too

Dated 2026-10-27 (branch `fix/ps270-garage-depth`, stacked on PR #306 at `35be2b1b`). The card: the
rule judged a garage's width only, so the row-6 house's garage, about 3000 mm clear along the line of
its west vehicle door, passed (QH5 in `audits/2026-10-26-first-contact-rerun-row6.md`; OH5, CH5, NH4,
RH5 before).

## What shipped

- No new code. `perRoomRules` (`src/lint/rules/per-room.ts`) gained a second `W_GARAGE_TOO_NARROW`
  finding, appended at the end of the per-room sequence (after `W_ROOM_TOO_NARROW`), so no existing
  output reorders. `garageClearDepth` reads, for each `door garage` on a rectangular garage's
  perimeter, the distance between the inner faces of the walls on the door's edge and on the edge
  opposite (`innerFaceOfRoomEdge`, the drawn edge where no wall backs one), from the room's own
  rectangle. With several vehicle doors the deepest axis is judged (a car can drive in through any).
  At a corner the door's host wall's run decides its edge.
- New ruleset field `garageDepthMm` (`src/lint/ruleset.ts`): default 5000, `uk-residential-advisory`
  6000, `null` turns the finding off. `CAR_BAY_WIDTH_MM` is unchanged (2700).
- Message, room named as a person would, no id: `The garage is 3000 mm deep from its vehicle door,
  between its wall faces, under the 5000 mm of clear depth a parked car needs — 2000 mm short.`
  Hints: the source of the figure (only for 5000 and 6000, the two cited), then the remedy, quoting
  the room id and the vehicle door's id when the author wrote one.
- Coverage: a garage with no `door garage` on its perimeter files the new reason `no-vehicle-door`
  (family `rooms`); a circle garage files `non-rectangular-room` (a polygon one already did, from the
  width check). Under `garageDepthMm: null` nothing is filed.
- Catalogue text (summary, cause, fix) names the depth finding; `docs/error-codes.md` and
  `llms-full.txt` through `gen:all`. `docs/analysis.md` lists the skip reason and the profile row;
  `docs/language-reference.md` names the figure. Stage stays `rooms`, layer `validity`.

## Sources (from `2026-10-27-ps267-271-sources.md` § PS2.70, nothing added)

- 5000 mm: Buckinghamshire Council, *Parking guidance for new developments* (2022) §8 Table 9, a car
  parking space 5.0 × 2.8 m [V]; Monmouthshire County Council, *SPG Domestic Garages* (2013) ¶21, a
  car port at least 5.0 m long [V].
- 6000 mm (`uk-residential-advisory`): Monmouthshire SPG (2013) ¶12 and ¶25, a garage's clear
  internal dimensions at least 3.0 × 6.0 m, citing Manual for Streets Appendix A note 3 [V];
  Buckinghamshire §9 Table 11, a garage 6.0 m long [V].
- `cn-` and `us-residential-advisory`: no figure found; they keep the default.

## Decisions

- **Same code, not `W_GARAGE_TOO_SHALLOW`.** The card's title and the brief: one garage rule, two
  measures.
- **No vehicle door: skip, not the long side.** The sources record suggested falling back to the
  room's long side; the executor brief asks for a declared skip, which is what shipped. A garage
  with no vehicle door is still judged for width.
- **The uk width (3000 per bay) is not adopted.** The record notes it; the brief keeps
  `CAR_BAY_WIDTH_MM` and adds no width field.
- **Clear depth, between wall faces,** where the width check reads the drawn short side: the sources
  give clear internal dimensions, and half of each wall is a large share of the margin.
