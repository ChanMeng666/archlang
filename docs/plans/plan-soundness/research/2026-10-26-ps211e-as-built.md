## Decisions

- **PS2.46, its own code.** Not `W_FURNITURE_OVER_WINDOW` with a second catalogue set: a hob is a
  low unit and blocks no light, the defect is fire and reach, and the remedy differs (put the sink
  under the window). One code per distinct check (cards §PS2.11e).
- **Every window is judged.** ArchLang does not model whether a window opens, which guideline 20
  turns on; a high window (sill ≥ 2100 mm) is excluded, as for the tall unit.
- **No dwelling reading (D6) for the hob rules.** The hazard is the hob's, whoever cooks on it, and
  RC4 (a café) is an acceptance row.
- **Any tall unit beside the hob, not only a fridge.** NKBA's landing area is blocked by any
  full-height unit; the wardrobe and bookcase are in `isTallUnit` for the same reason. A wall beside
  the hob (A7, RS3's side, RH2) would read the same guideline: filed as PS2.59.
- **The `double` kind was paid for.** Its spec text took 74 UTF-16 units, 28 of them paid back by cutting
  the door line's drawing-only aside "(nothing measured reads it)" after `open` is DRAWING-only (net +46);
  SKILL gained two sentences; the catalogue's `E_DOOR_KIND_CLAUSE` and `E_DOOR_KIND_CURVED` say
  where it stands. The law: 25 plans that write no `double` door, pinned against `8c2d6150`'s
  `src/` (whole payload, every storey, with diagnostics), are byte-identical; `format` and Plan JSON
  round-trip it. Without the kind, `W_LEAF_TOO_WIDE`'s only clean remedies were a sliding door or a
  narrower one: the C2 pier and eight wide leaves in the examples are the shape it replaces.
- **Wardrobe front ≥ 600: nothing more ships here.** `clear front 600` is the declaration (PS3.7,
  `W_FURN_CLEAR_FRONT`); a default is ruled out (cards §PS2.11e, PS3.7: `garden-loft` cannot hold
  one). A profile figure needs a source; none read so far gives a wardrobe front, though Approved
  Document M4(2)'s bed access zones would catch OA4, CA4 and RH4 from the bed's side: card PS2.60.
- **PS2.53 left open.** No sourced per-family depth ceiling for a wardrobe or bookcase was found.

## The face datum and the eval judge (D25)

(dims-auto-cottage and galley-kitchen by the face datum, compact-studio by the opening reading).

fixture's `notes`, `JUDGE_VERSION` unchanged, recorded as D25 *awaiting ratification*; its reverse-if
returns `W_DOORWAY_BLOCKED` alone to the centreline.
