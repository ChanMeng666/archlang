# PS4.3a `keep`: as built

## What shipped

- **The statement.** `keep <form> [hard|soft|advisory]`, seven forms (design §2), parsed by
  `parseKeep` (`src/parser.ts`) from the word tables `KEEP_WORDS`/`KEEP_GRADES`
  (`src/grammar/tokens.ts`); `KeepNode` (`src/ast.ts`); printed by `statement-print.ts`
  (`keepFormText` is also the `keep` text every report quotes). `keep` joins `KEYWORDS.control`
  and `STATEMENT_STARTS`; it joins `LEVEL_SHARED_KINDS` so a root keep of a multi-storey plan
  reaches the resolver (which refuses every form but `stacked` there) instead of `E_LEVEL_MIX`.
- **Expansion** (`expandKeep`, `src/keep.ts`, called from `expandScopeFrame`): refused inside a
  component (`ExpandCtx.keepPlace`); `stacked` refused anywhere but the root of a plan of two or
  more storeys; charged one step per target and level before the record exists; counted
  plan-wide against `MAX_KEEPS` (the count and the raised flag chain across storeys through
  `ResolveExtras`); `<mm>` and levels evaluated on the meter; `E_KEEP_SELF`, `E_KEEP_VALUE`.
- **Measurement** (`evaluateKeeps`, `evaluateStacked`): step 8 of `resolveBody`, after every other
  step; skipped on a `paper` plan's geometry probes (`ResolveExtras.probe`, R11). Root keeps leave
  `shared` in `levelPlanFor`; root `stacked` keeps are expanded by the last storey's drawn pass
  against the plan-level bindings and measured in `resolveLevelsImpl` after the last storey, on the
  same counter (R12, R13); a crossing there is `E_STEP_LIMIT` and every storey is left unresolved.
  Results live in side tables keyed by the storey's `ResolvedPlan` (`keepStateOfIr`,
  `stackedResultsOfIr`), read by `keepResultsOf`; nothing is written to an element or the AST.
- **Exactness.** Every quantity is an integer count of half-lattice units (0.0005 mm): an edge,
  a length or a `<mm>` is `2·L(v)`, a centre line `L(2v)`, `L(v) = sign(v)·round(|v|·1000)`; a
  wall face is `2·L(c) ± L(t)`. Datums are grid-snapped with the plan's own `snap`; `<mm>` never.
  Residuals print through `fmtHalfLattice` (`src/num-format.ts`, digits only).
- **Reports.** `W_KEEP_VIOLATED` (compile channel, hard only) after every other diagnostic of its
  storey, `stacked` after the storeys'; `describe().keeps[]` last; `validate --json` `keeps`
  before `next`; `next` names a broken soft keep in tier `intent` after the gating assertions and
  the `--graph` mismatch (`NextInput.keeps`). Seven catalogue entries with executed examples.
- **Never-worse counts (R7).** `finish`/`furnish` count compile codes already (`codesOf`), so a
  broken hard keep is a gained code; `repair`'s no-worse law now enters each broken hard keep as a
  conflict keyed `(level, text, occurrence)` whose refs are its targets, so a moved piece a keep
  names is pinned; `arch fix` rolls back a pass that raises the `W_KEEP_VIOLATED` count (unless
  `--force`); `reroll`'s proof already compares `compile().diagnostics` and `describe()` (the
  `keeps` key included). Each is proven in `test/keep.test.ts`.
- **Teaching.** The spec line (`STATEMENT_GRAMMAR.keep`, interpolated from the tables), the four
  cuts of R4, one SKILL sentence (step 8), a `language-reference.md` section ("Keep — declared
  invariants"), `docs/error-codes.md` and every generated artifact regenerated.
- **Tests.** `test/keep-byte-identity.test.ts` (K1 over 269 files against the baseline committed
  first; K2 over the 10 accepted corpus plans), `test/keep-digests.test.ts` (K3, 14 rows, pinned
  last), `test/keep.test.ts` (40 cases: the §9 drill through the CLI, per-form rules, R5/R6/R18/R20,
  parsing, the printer round trip, Plan JSON's drop, the step charges, R7, id-free messages, D4),
  `test/keep-corpus/` (14 plans), `test/spec-forms.test.ts` (one snippet per form and grade, and
  `W_KEEP_VIOLATED` in the negative corpus).

## Measurements

- **Spec** (`renderLlmSpec().length`, UTF-16): 29,729 before, 29,757 after (headroom 243). The
  keep line is 425 units with its newline (the design's 432 less `/impact`, see decision 2); the
  four cuts are 397.
- **Step charge per form** (a two-room storey with one wall: elements 3, walls 1, entries 3;
  `test/keep.test.ts` pins them): `align` 15, `equal` 15, `on axis` 15, `fixed` 23, `offset` 25,
  `min clear` 64, `stacked` over two storeys 24. Each is the expansion (1 for the statement, 1 per
  target, the value's expression and its scope copy), the storey's id index built once
  (elements + walls + 1) and authored-id set (entries + 1), and the measure (`1 + targets`; `min
  clear` `1 + segments` plus its scan built once: walls + segments + 2·segments² + 1; `stacked`
  `1 + 4 × levels` plus each storey's index). A second keep on the same storey pays only its
  expansion and its measure.
- **K1:** 269 files (34 examples, 25 eval goldens, 146 defect-corpus, 22 taste-corpus, 36
  first-contact plans, 6 repros), 269/269 digests and step counts unchanged.
- **Corpus movement:** none. No example changed; no `lint()`, `describe()`, `validate --json` or
  `impact --json` byte of any keep-free plan moved; `eval/judge-fixture.json` untouched.

## Decisions taken here (the design was silent or the brief re-scoped)

1. **Scope follows the brief, not design §11's split.** `validate --json` `keeps`/`next`, the
   never-worse counts, the spec line and its cuts and the SKILL sentence are in A; `--facts
   depends` rows, `impact`'s keeps section and PS4.11's Plan JSON note are left to B.
2. **The spec line says "advisory in describe/validate"**, not "/impact": impact's keeps section is
   B's. B adds the word (+7 units, within the headroom). Likewise the SKILL sentence says `impact
   --set` lists a broken hard keep "among the diagnostics a change breaks": until B's
   `sideDiagnostics` filter, `W_KEEP_VIOLATED` reaches impact through its compile channel.
3. **Levels are expressions (R14)**, so `on levels 1 -1` is one level, `1 - 1`, and refused by the
   parser (fewer than two levels); the printer parenthesises a later level that does not start
   with a digit (`1 (-1)`), and the reference says so.
4. **Highlighting.** The form words `equal`, `depth`, `stacked`, `levels`, `fixed` join
   `KEYWORDS.attribute` and the grades `KEYWORDS.enum` (no new category, R14; `min` is not added,
   it stays the built-in). The spec's clause partition (`CLAUSE_ATTRIBUTES`) names them; the GBNF
   gains `keep-*` productions rendered from the tables with a guard.
5. **Naming walls and openings in the resolver.** Lint's `wallNamed` needs a `LintContext`, so
   `src/naming.ts` gains the neutral `wallRunPhrase` (rooms on the two faces, else the place), read
   at the run's midpoint and, where that lands on a room boundary, its quarter points; an opening is
   named by probes at its own position ("the window of "Living"", "the door between …").
6. **`min clear` and the joined outline.** Building the joinery in the resolver is ruled out
   (iron law), so a segment end is read as joined when another wall's segment runs through or ends
   at it (a ring's corner, a T-junction): the body then runs on by half its own thickness. That
   reproduces design §9 and R18 exactly (3850/4800, then 3400/5000 with `skipped_walls: 2`).
7. **Points are not snapped.** An opening's centre and a piece's footprint centre are read as
   resolved; only datums snap (R5 speaks of datums).
8. **Compass words** in a keep follow the datum rule (root of a `north up` plan only; keeps never
   sit in a component), refused with the existing `E_DATUM_COMPASS`.
9. **D4 law scope.** Stated over the corpus plans that are single-storey, name no axis, hold
   literal values and have no half-grid centre (`offset-fixed`, `min-clear`, `instance`,
   `align-equal`): a frame carries no positioning axis, and grid snapping rounds a negative half
   value the other way (`Math.round`), so a half-grid centre is not equivariant by construction.
   `transformKeep` takes the wall orientations and which ids are openings from the plan as written.
10. **ADR 0033 is Accepted here**, as its own status line says ("to be accepted when the PR that
    builds the statement and its law (PS4.3a) merges"); decision 6 is noted as PS4.3b's.
11. **Trailers.** Commits carry the session's actual model in `Co-Authored-By` (Claude Opus 5.5),
    not the common brief's line.

## Not honoured, or left open

- Nothing of the design was bent around a law. Plan JSON says nothing yet about the keeps it
  drops (PS4.11, B per the brief); the drop itself is pinned (`test/keep.test.ts`).
- The K3 table includes `validate --json`, so a later lint rewording on a corpus plan moves its
  row; re-measure that row with its reason, as `docs/testing.md` §3 says.
