## PS2.10

The card's as-built note was already moved, verbatim, to `2026-10-24-ps0-ps2-as-built.md` § PS2.10.

From the PR body (#243):
- **Rule.** A text reads as a length when it is wholly digits, an optional decimal part and an
  optional unit `mm`, `cm` or `m`; a bare number is millimetres. Comma-grouped thousands only with
  no unit; a bare `4.000`/`12.500` is not read (a European thousands dot, or metres without the
  `m`), `4.000 m` is. Not checked: `VARIES`, `EQ`, `R9000`, `φ5000`, `~4000`, `2300 clear`,
  `2000 arc`, signs, numbers finer than the 0.001 mm lattice, numbers past 2^53 µm.
- **Rounding.** Read at its own written precision, `2·|span − written| ≤ one unit of the last
  digit`, decided in integers on the 0.001 mm lattice (ADR 0022), ties accepted; a text copied
  from the drawing never fires (a test asserts it against the printed SVG text).
- **Fix.** `maybe-incorrect` (needs `fix --unsafe`), because the line can be the wrong side
  (gallery-l was); none for an interpolated text or a statement drawn at several spans.
- **Placement.** Appended last to `LINT_RULES` (family `annotation`), `RULE_CLASS` `invariant`, on
  in every profile; layer `drafting` (PS5.1a's rebase note).
- **Corpus.** Three example dims fixed, never blessed: gallery-l's entrance (geometry wrong) and
  aquarium's two arc windows (both wrong; text `2.0m`). Only their SVGs moved; the swap-back proof
