# PS2.66 as built: a bathroom with a WC and no basin

Dated 2026-10-27 (branch `fix/ps266-bath-no-basin`, stacked on PR #297 at `bd669887`). The card: the
row-6 house's family bathroom holds a bathtub and a WC and no basin, and no rule said so (QH7 in
`audits/2026-10-26-first-contact-rerun-row6.md`).

## What shipped

- No new code. `W_ROOM_MISSING_DEFINING` (`src/lint/rules/room-contents.ts`) gained a second
  reading, `WASH_WITH` in `src/lint/defining.ts`: a `bath` room that holds a `wc` also needs a
  `basin`. Both categories are looked up in the bath row of `FURNISH_TABLE`, never retyped, and a
  guard in `test/lint-room-contents.test.ts` holds them to the row. `DEFINING` is unchanged, so
  `finish`, `W_ROOM_NO_FIXTURE`'s hint and the taste criteria read what they read before.
- Message, room named by label, no id: `"Bathroom" is furnished (bathtub, wc) but has no basin: a
  room with a WC needs one to wash hands at, in the room or in one a door joins it to (not a
  kitchen).` A bath that lacks its bathtub or shower too keeps its old message (the basin is asked once the
  bath is defined), so a plan the old reading already reported moves no digest. The hint quotes the basin statement, checked by
  `pasteSet`, as before.
- Catalogue text of the code (and `docs/error-codes.md` through `gen:all`) names the reading. Stage
  `fixtures`, layer `validity`, family `contents`: the rule's own, unchanged.

## Decisions

- **Extend, not a new code.** The finding is about the pieces a furnished room must hold, read from
  the same furnish row, with the same hint machinery and the same `finish` never-worse exemption; only
  the message tail differs, and the rule now builds it per reason.
- **Source.** England's Approved Document G (2015), requirement G4 and its guidance on washing
  facilities: a washbasin in the room containing the WC, or in a room or space giving direct access
  to it, provided that room is not used to prepare food.
- **The *Open* case: the adjoining-room reading.** A basin counts when it stands (footprint centre on
  the floor, as every piece is read) in the bath, or in any room joined to the bath directly by a door
  or a cased opening (an edge of `ctx.access()`) whose uses do not include `kitchen`. That is AD G's
  rule, wider than the brief's suggested bath/wc/dressing list: ArchLang has no `dressing` use, and
  AD G allows any access space that is not a kitchen (a bedroom vanity basin serving an ensuite WC is
  compliant). A wall with no door in it does not count, and neither does a room two doors away.
  Tested both ways in `test/lint-room-contents.test.ts`.
- **A `wc` room is not asked.** The card keeps a cloakroom defined by its WC alone; `WASH_WITH` has a
  `bath` entry only, pinned by the guard.
- **`finish` cannot disagree.** The bath row marks `wc` and `basin` required, so `finish` writes both
  or leaves the bath out; it never writes a bath this reading reports.
