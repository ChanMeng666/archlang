## What the batch measured

- **`finish`:** 1,384 single-storey plans, 294 furnished, 1,059 pieces, 0 sources moved against a `git archive` of the base.
  Time grows faster than storeys: on one floor of seven rooms, about 2 s for 1 storey, 18 s for 4, 35–58 s for 8, because
  each gate check compiles and lints the whole building.
- **J5's field** moved `describe()` on 719 plans, each byte-identical with the key removed, and 69 of 323 K1 rows.
- **J2:** no corpus plan builds an exterior wall off a ring, so the new half ran on derived plans: 754 storeys mutated at
  every side, 0 hits on 11,768 garden, boundary and fin cases, and of 8,994 annex-plus-gap cases 8,320 name both ends.
- **J4:** of 1,377 corpus names, 25 rows moved, reports outside two hostile fixtures went from 65 to 36, and two labels with
  no earlier report moved, both true overflows hidden by the flat 0.5 em. `examples/corner-cafe` "Lobby" was on a door's arc.

## What the red-team rounds caught

Each PR had one round, J4 two. All were on merged-looking green branches, and these were the ones that mattered.

- **J4:** the AFM guard never ran. `pdfkit/package.json` is not an exported path, so the test took its "absent" branch on
  every machine and would have failed in CI; the executor had reported it as reading the AFM. Then a `-6e-16` from
  `rectAreaOutside` put a label 450 mm off-centre, and after that fix a one-ulp tie still flipped a label 1.8 m on a 1 mm edit.
- **J2:** three regressions against main from the new reading: any wall below storey height read as a parapet, one label
  point deciding enclosure, and an open-air gate on `label` only.
- **J1:** the sentence blamed a shaft that did not reach the storey, and the hint did not cure.
- **J5:** a hint quoted a demolished wall; an open boundary was called a partition and sent to a `between` that refuses.
- **J6's CI, not the red team:** `test/block-stair-byte-identity.test.ts` hashes through a helper, so a grep of `*.test.ts`
  for hash words missed it (gotchas).

## What was decided against the card

- **Browser widths (J4).** The executor fitted to Roboto alone. Reversed by the main session: the SVG is read in a browser,
  where Helvetica or Arial at weight 600 draws about 7 % wider, so a label could lint clean and cross a wall.
- **#364's workaround root** stays unfurnished: a placed component's rooms are left to the author on both plan shapes (D33).
