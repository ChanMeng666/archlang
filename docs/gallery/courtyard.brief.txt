Design a single-storey U-shaped courtyard house of about 130 m²: three wings wrap around an open courtyard that is open on its fourth side. One side wing holds three bedrooms (at least 10 m² each) and a bathroom along a corridor; the middle wing holds the entrance hall and a living room of at least 28 m²; the other side wing holds a kitchen/dining room of at least 20 m² and a utility room. The living room and every bedroom must have a window facing the courtyard or the outside.
