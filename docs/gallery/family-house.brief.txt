Design a single-storey four-bedroom family house of about 160 m². Rooms: an entrance hall, a living room of at least 25 m², a kitchen of at least 15 m², a dining room of at least 12 m² directly connected to the kitchen, a study of at least 8 m², a utility room, a master bedroom of at least 15 m² with an en-suite, three further bedrooms of at least 10 m² each, a family bathroom, and a separate WC near the entrance. Every bedroom, the study and the living room need a window.
