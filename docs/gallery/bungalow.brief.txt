Design a single-storey three-bedroom bungalow of about 110 m². Rooms: an entrance hall, an open-plan kitchen/dining/living space of at least 35 m² (draw it as one room), a master bedroom of at least 14 m² with its own en-suite shower room reached only from the master bedroom, two further bedrooms of at least 10 m² each, a family bathroom, and a utility room reached from the kitchen/living space. Every bedroom needs a window.
