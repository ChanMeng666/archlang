Design the ground floor of a single-storey house on a narrow plot: the whole building must fit inside a rectangle 5.5 m wide by 16 m long (outer faces of walls). It needs a hall with the front door on one short end, a living room of at least 16 m², a kitchen of at least 8 m², one bedroom of at least 10 m², and a bathroom. Windows may only go on the two short ends, because the long sides are party walls; the bedroom and living room each still need a window.
